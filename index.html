01 · PDF upload & reading order

PDF bytes are processed in your browser. Scanned PDFs without selectable text require a separate approved transcription/OCR process.

No PDF processed.

02 · Source text / review

Manual check required

For mixed-language PDFs, choose English page(s) and then Arabic page(s) separately. Page selection prevents extracting the wrong language into the XML. Read each PDF column in its entirety before the next column.

Automatic extraction may change paragraph boundaries, misread Arabic font characters, or lose tables. Do not assume that text extraction proves medical accuracy.

03 · Approved PIL sections & LOINC mapping

Six coded sections

Use headings and text from the approved source. For non-standard leaflets (such as Bonteo's SmPC-style layout), manually allocate the verified text to each section. The application never fabricates missing medical information.

04 · Clinical tables

Produces real XHTML <table option-scroll="yes" option-preset="strips-rows"> with <thead>, <tbody> and <td>, following the Zithromax display pattern. Tables are not converted to images.

05 · Verified regulatory & product information

Do not copy an unrelated medicine's ATC, administration route or supply codes from the demonstration XML. Confirm each value with Regulatory Affairs.

Stable identifier registry

Only identifiers are stored in this browser (no PIL text). Download a registry backup and reuse it when changing browsers/computers. Import existing approved IDs instead of generating new ones.

06 · Quality checks & download

Output is a draft unless both review boxes are checked. The JFDA debugger checks XML structure; it cannot establish that medical statements match the approved PDF.
